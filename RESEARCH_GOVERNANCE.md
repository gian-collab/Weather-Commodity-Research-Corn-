# Research Governance

1. Reanalysis is ex-post verification/physical data only.
2. Forecast-market experiments use only archived point-in-time forecasts.
3. Finalized annual yield experiments use crop years <= 2025 in this release; 2026 is excluded.
4. Model selection is based on research OOS folds; reserved latest finalized years remain separately identified.
5. Geography uses prior-year USDA production weights where available.
6. Crop-stage windows are frozen before results; no stage window may be moved after inspecting P&L.
7. Return and volatility horizons are frozen at the configured grid before results.
8. All strategy/economic conclusions must report sample size, OOS metric and data limitations.
9. Public continuous futures are a proxy; do not describe results as official CME settlement backtests.
10. Deterministic run-revision instability is not ensemble spread. Ensemble uncertainty is prospective until enough AIFS-ENS snapshots are collected.
11. Null and adverse findings remain in the article evidence bundle.
